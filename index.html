Hyejin Yoo

M.S. Student in Data Science · Seoul National University

I am a graduate researcher in the VLDB Lab at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Sang–won Lee. I work on database systems, with a focus on transaction processing and concurrency control for scalable OLTP systems.

My current research explores how to reduce synchronization bottlenecks under write–write contention while preserving strong transactional guarantees.

Research interests: Database Systems · Transaction Processing · Concurrency Control · OLTP Systems · Storage Systems

Research

I am broadly interested in building high-performance transaction processing systems that scale on modern hardware. My recent work studies write–write conflicts in OLTP systems and investigates how database mechanisms originally designed for recovery can be repurposed to improve concurrency.
